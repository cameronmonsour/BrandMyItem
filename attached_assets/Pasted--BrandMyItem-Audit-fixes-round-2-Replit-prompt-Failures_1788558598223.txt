## 1. Owner posting flow

1.1 **Spot pricing is owner-editable — wrong model.** Remove the "Price each brand spot" step and the `cuSpotPrice*` inputs. Prices are computed, never typed: each spot's price = (its sized area ÷ total sized area) × campaign total, rounded to whole dollars, with the rounding remainder added to the largest spot so the sum equals the campaign total exactly. Show the computed prices read-only on the sizing preview ("Spot 1 · $241"). Remove the validation messages "Complete sizing and price every sponsor spot before posting" and "Every spot needs a price and the spot total must match the full purchase goal"; replace with a single "Complete sizing before posting" if no spots are sized.

1.2 **Validation feedback is invisible.** Clicking "Post my item live" currently scrolls the page to the top and shows a toast at the bottom, off-screen. Do not scroll to top. Show each validation error inline, directly under the field it concerns, in red ink, and scroll *that field* into view. Keep the toast only as a secondary notice. Applies to: character not selected, missing name/email/address, address format, assent unchecked, purpose > 500 chars, social required, W-9 required.

1.3 **$2,000 gate — complete it.** For any listing with campaign total ≥ $2,000:
- Show a visible block above the assent checkbox titled "Required for listings of $2,000 or more" containing (a) a W-9 upload (PDF) and (b) at least one linked social handle. The block appears the moment the campaign total crosses $2,000, not on submit.
- Both are required before "Post my item live" is enabled. Inline errors per 1.2 if attempted without them.
- Copy: "Add at least one public social handle for a listing of $2,000 or more." (not "campaign").

1.4 **Social linking uses `window.prompt()`.** Replace with an inline text field that appears under the button when clicked ("@handle"), validates the handle format per network, and shows the linked handle as a removable chip. No native dialogs anywhere in the app.

1.5 **Structured shipping address.** Replace the single `pAddress` line with fields: street, unit (optional), city, state (select, two-letter), ZIP (5 digits). Reject PO boxes (`/\bP\.?\s*O\.?\s*BOX\b/i`) with "We can't ship to PO boxes." Remove the regex-on-one-line validation and its message.

1.6 **Scroll/paint glitch.** After the scroll jump the sticky nav rendered mid-viewport with a blank block above it. Remove the programmatic scroll-to-top on submit (1.2) and verify the nav stays pinned at top at 603px, 1440px, and 390px.
