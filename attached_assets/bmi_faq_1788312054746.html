Asked and answered.

Everything owners and brands ask before they use BrandMyItem, in plain language. BrandMyItem is operated by IRLi LLC.

The basics

Is this real?

Yes. Brands pay for ad spots on an item someone wants. When every spot is claimed, BrandMyItem buys that item new, at retail, and ships it to the owner. The owner carries it with the brands applied. No points, no credits, no gift cards.

Who actually buys the item?

BrandMyItem does. Once a listing is fully funded, we purchase the item new from a retailer and ship it directly to the owner. The owner never receives or handles any money at any point.

Does the owner make money?

No. The owner never receives cash. The only thing an owner ever receives is the item itself. Brand payments are made to BrandMyItem for the placement service, not to the owner.

Is this crowdfunding?

No. Brands are buying a defined advertising placement from BrandMyItem at a fixed price, and every purchase is automatically refunded if the listing doesn't fully fund within 60 days. Nothing is pledged, nothing is held, and no one is asked to fund a promise.

How are spots priced?

By surface. Each spot's price is its share of the item's usable surface multiplied by the item's retail price, and all spot prices on a listing add up to that retail price exactly. Nothing above it, nothing below it.

What items are allowed?

Items from the current catalog only: everyday, publicly visible items of $300 or more with a real ad surface, like laptops, phones, luggage, golf bags, and coolers. Nothing motorized, registered, or insured is listed, ever.

For owners

What do I commit to?

Three things: keep the branding applied for your full term, use the item in public the way it's meant to be used, and send one check-in photo on the schedule you chose. That's the entire commitment.

How long is the term?

You choose 6, 12, or 18 months when you post the item. The term locks for everyone the moment the first spot sells.

How often do I check in?

You pick the pace when you post: weekly, biweekly, or monthly. One photo per cycle showing the item and its branding in use, for the whole term. No dashboard, no login required to keep it going.

What happens if I miss check-ins?

Missed check-ins put a public flag on your listing, and continued misses restrict you from posting new items. There is no financial penalty and nothing is taken from you; your record is simply public.

Who owns the item?

You do, from the day it's delivered. The term is a display commitment, not a loan or a lease. You agree not to sell the item or remove the branding until your term ends.

When does my item arrive?

If you're applying the branding yourself: we purchase and ship within about 23 days of full funding, and you apply the materials and submit your first check-in within 7 days of delivery, about 30 days end to end. If you chose assisted application (+10%), it arrives pre-branded within 60 days.

Who pays the +10% assisted fee?

Not you. If you choose assisted application when posting, the fee is built into the listing's campaign total that brands fund. Owners never pay anything on BrandMyItem.

Is my address shared with brands?

No. Your shipping address is used by BrandMyItem only, to deliver the item. Brands see your listing, your check-in photos, and the public record, never your address or contact details.

Do I have to say it's sponsored if I post about it?

Yes. If you post about the item on social media, FTC endorsement rules require you to clearly disclose the material connection, a plain "#ad" or "sponsored" does it. The physical branding on the item itself is already self-evident and needs no extra label.

Do I owe taxes on the item?

Receiving an item in exchange for a display commitment may be taxable income depending on your situation. You're responsible for any taxes that apply to you, and BrandMyItem issues tax forms where the law requires them.

What if my item arrives damaged or gets lost?

Items are purchased new at retail, so the manufacturer's warranty applies from day one. If a shipment arrives damaged or never arrives, contact us and we'll resolve it with the retailer and carrier; that part is on us, not you.

For brands

What does a brand actually get?

A physical placement, your logo as a sticker, patch, embroidery, or engraving, on a real item carried in public for the listing's full term, plus a proof record: the purchase receipt, the shipment, and every one of the owner's check-in photos, all in one place.

How does payment work?

You pay the listed spot price in full at checkout, by card. There are no invoices, no deposits, and no escrow. If the listing doesn't fully fund within 60 days, your entire payment comes back automatically.

What if the item never sells out?

Every listing has a 60-day funding window. If every spot isn't claimed by day 60, every buyer is refunded in full, automatically, to the original payment method. No request, no waiting, no partial outcomes.

Can I cancel a spot I bought?

Spot purchases are final once made. The 60-day automatic refund is your protection: if the campaign doesn't fund, you get everything back without asking.

What do I submit at checkout?

One production-ready logo file, your brand name, a work email, and a destination link. That's the whole submission; it travels with the order to application.

Can any brand join? What's not allowed?

Any business with the rights to its logo and a lawful product can buy a spot, first come, first served. We decline placements we can't lawfully or safely apply, including marks you don't own, and content that is unlawful, adult, hateful, or in regulated categories such as tobacco and vaping.

Who owns the logo, and what are you allowed to do with it?

You do. By purchasing, you confirm you have the rights to the mark and license BrandMyItem to reproduce it once, on that item, and for the owner's check-in photos of the item to be shown publicly. Nothing more.

What keeps the owner honest?

Structure, mostly. The owner never touches the money, BrandMyItem controls the purchase and shipping, and the owner's check-in record is public, missed check-ins flag the listing and restrict future postings. You can see exactly how a listing is performing at any time.

Do I get impressions or analytics?

No, and that's the point. This is physical placement in the real world, not a feed. Your measurable record is the proof itself: where your logo is, that it shipped, and the recurring photos showing it in use.

Can one brand buy multiple spots?

Yes. Any open spot is available to any eligible brand, including buying every spot on an item, which funds it instantly.

What happens if an owner breaks the commitment after funding?

Funded campaigns are final, and the check-in record documents performance publicly. Owners who stop checking in are flagged and restricted from posting again, so the system is built to make follow-through the only good outcome for them.

Can I reuse the owner's photos in my own ads?

Check-in photos exist as proof of placement on your record. If you republish them as your own advertising, standard advertising rules apply to you as they would to any ad you run, including truthful presentation and any required disclosures.
