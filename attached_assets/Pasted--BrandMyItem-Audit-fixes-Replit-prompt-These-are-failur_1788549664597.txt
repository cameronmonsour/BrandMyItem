## 1. Reservation flow (site cannot take a reservation)

1.1 **Logo upload gate is broken.** `POST /api/sponsor-reservation-drafts/{id}/logo/request-url` returns `400 {"error":"Logo metadata is not permitted"}` for a clean, metadata-free PNG. The request body is only `{name, size, contentType}`. Find the check that produces this error and fix it so a valid file passes. Per Part D, the uploader should accept **SVG or PDF only** (max 20 MB); reject other types with the message "Please upload your logo as an SVG or PDF." Do not reject on metadata. If you strip metadata server-side, strip it silently.

1.2 **Failures are silent.** When any step of the reserve flow fails (draft, logo, checkout session), show the server's error text inside the modal under the button, in red ink, and re-enable the button. Never leave the modal open with no feedback.

1.3 **Draft lock has no exit.** A failed attempt leaves the spot locked ("Placement is already being reserved", 409) for ~10 minutes. On any failure after draft creation, delete the draft (`DELETE /api/sponsor-reservation-drafts/{id}`) so the spot reopens immediately. Also release the draft when the modal is cancelled or closed.

1.4 **Modal survives navigation.** The reserve modal stayed open after changing routes (`#item/…` → `#home`). Close and release the draft on any hash change.

1.5 **Stripe must be reachable and in test mode.** After 1.1, a complete reservation must create a Stripe Checkout Session in `mode: "setup"` (no charge) and redirect to it. Confirm the publishable/secret keys in Replit Secrets are `pk_test_` / `sk_test_` for this environment. Expose `GET /api/health` returning `{ok:true, stripeMode:"test"|"live", resend:true|false}` (no keys).

## 6. Legal documents (`legal.html`)

Add to **Terms of Use**:
- Breach clause: "If you sell, gift, trade, or transfer the item, or intentionally remove, cover, deface, or alter any placement during the term, you owe IRLi LLC the listing's campaign total plus reasonable costs of collection. IRLi may pursue this by demand letter, small claims court, or a collection agency." (Do not use "liquidated damages.")
- Visibility: "Placements must remain visible whenever the item is in use. A case, skin, cover, or sleeve that hides a placement while the item is in use is treated as covering it."
- Wear: "Normal wear is not a breach. Report it through check-in and IRLi will replace or reapply at no cost."
- Theft/loss: "If the item is stolen or lost, file a police report and upload it. The term ends and you owe nothing."
- $2,000 gate: "Listings with a campaign total of $2,000 or more require a completed W-9 and one linked social account before publishing."
- Brand make-good: "If a placement fails during the term for reasons attributable to the owner, IRLi will place you on a comparable item for the remaining term at no charge. If no comparable spot is available within 30 days, IRLi refunds the unserved portion of the term. This make-good is your exclusive remedy, and you agree not to initiate a chargeback for any placement covered by it."

Add to **Refund Policy**: the make-good section (comparable spot first, prorated refund after 30 days, formula: spot price × months remaining ÷ term months).

Add to **Check-in Policy**: what a photo must show (item in use, all placements visible), the "Report wear" option, the "Report theft or loss" option and its effect.

Add to **Privacy Policy**: W-9 data (including TIN) collected for listings ≥ $2,000, stored encrypted, used only for tax reporting; police reports retained for the term plus 3 years; Stripe as processor for saved payment methods.

## 8. Assets and performance

8.1 Add `alt` to every product image: item name plus finish (e.g., "MacBook Pro, Silver"). 66 of 70 images currently have none.

8.2 Convert product PNGs to **WebP** (AVIF where the toolchain allows) at a max of **1600px** on the long side; 12 images are over 2000px. Keep PNG only where transparency at sticker edges visibly degrades.

8.3 `loading="lazy"` on every image below the fold; `fetchpriority="high"` on the hero item images; explicit `width` and `height` on every `<img>`.

8.4 three.js (r128) loads on every page. Load it only on the route that uses it, or remove it if the 5 canvases can be CSS.

8.5 `og:image` currently points to `https://brandmyitem.com/og-default.svg`. Generate a **1200×630 PNG**, serve it from the current origin (relative URL resolved by the server), and set `og:image`, `twitter:image`, `twitter:card=summary_large_image`. SVG is not accepted by X, LinkedIn, or iMessage.
