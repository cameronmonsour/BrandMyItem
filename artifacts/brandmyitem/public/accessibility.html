Accessibility

BrandMyItem is committed to making our marketplace usable by as many people as possible. We use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, labels for form controls, and descriptive text alternatives for meaningful images.

Need help?

If you encounter a barrier, email accessibility@brandmyitem.com. Include the page, the task you were trying to complete, and your browser or assistive technology if you are comfortable sharing it. We will work to provide the information or service in an accessible way.
