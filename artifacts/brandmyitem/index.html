BRAND
Is this real?
Completely. The items are real, the stickers are real die-cut vinyl, and every listing belongs to a real person who takes it where they work, study, and travel. The only fiction is the idea that a laptop lid or a suitcase isn't premium ad inventory.
Does the owner make money?
No, and that's the point. Once a listing is fully funded, IRLi LLC buys the item and ships it to the owner. Spot money funds that purchase, the platform fee runs the business, and cash never changes hands with the owner at any point.
What does a brand actually get?
Your logo printed on the item itself, visible everywhere it goes and in everything its owner posts, plus your spot on the listing page with a link to your site, for as long as the owner's chosen term runs (6, 12, or 18 months). No impressions promised, just real presence in real rooms your ads can't reach.
How does payment work?
Buying a spot charges your card immediately for the listed price plus the platform fee. No holds, no waiting. If a listing doesn't sell out within 60 days, every brand on it is refunded automatically. If it sells out but shipment runs late, you'll get a notice before the promised date passes, with the choice to wait or take a full refund. If the item can never be delivered, you're refunded in full without having to ask.
Can any brand join?
Almost. Every sponsor is approved by hand before anything is printed. Alcohol, cannabis, tobacco, and political ads aren't accepted, and the item's owner has the final say on what rides on their stuff. Refused purchases are refunded in full.
What keeps the owner honest?
Monthly photo check-ins, public on the listing for anyone to see. The first one requires a receipt dated after the listing funded, so nobody can claim credit for an item they already owned. Miss a check-in and the listing gets flagged publicly; miss two in a row and that owner can't post another listing.
What can I post?
Objects and wearables. Laptops, phones, tumblers, luggage, coolers, wedding suits, graduation gowns. Nothing that needs insurance or permits, so no cars or property.
What if the item never sells out?
Every listing gets 60 days. Sell out in time and it ships. If it doesn't, every brand who bought a spot is refunded automatically and the listing closes — no request needed, no money left sitting indefinitely.
Do I have to say it's sponsored if I post about it?
Yes. Any post about a sponsored item needs to disclose that, and BrandMyItem's built-in share tool tags it for you automatically. That's not a house rule — it's an FTC requirement any time you post about something you received for free in exchange for showing a brand's logo.
Who actually buys the item?
IRLi LLC does, once the listing reaches 100%. Neither the brand nor the owner fronts the item cost — brands pay for ad space, IRLi LLC buys and ships the physical item, and the owner never touches campaign money at any step.
