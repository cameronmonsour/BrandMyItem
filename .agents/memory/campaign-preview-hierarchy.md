---
name: Campaign preview hierarchy
description: Durable visual convention for the campaign preview modal.
---

Campaign preview details should read as one calm editorial surface. Use spacing, aligned facts, and thin section dividers to create hierarchy instead of placing every content group inside its own rounded gray card.

**Why:** Nested cards made the preview visually heavy and generic, obscuring the campaign title, owner context, and funding status that should lead the experience.

**How to apply:** Keep the product introduction prominent, separate brief and audience content with restrained rules, make funding the strongest numeric moment, and preserve a persistent but quiet action footer.
