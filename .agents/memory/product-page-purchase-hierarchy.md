---
name: Product-page purchase hierarchy
description: The durable presentation and interaction rules for live BrandMyItem product pages.
---

Live product pages should lead with a contained product stage, then pair campaign funding context with one explicit spot selector that works equally well with mouse, touch, and keyboard. Image hotspots are supplementary, not the only purchase path.

**Why:** The previous full-width product image overwhelmed the page, and small image hotspots made buying uncertain on mobile. The product should feel premium while the transaction stays immediate and unambiguous.

**How to apply:** Keep product imagery proportionate with generous surrounding space; show every spot with its number, price, status, placement metadata, and sponsor mark; route open rows to the existing checkout flow. Put detailed owner fit, fulfillment, verification, and protection content into a progressive product story below the decision area.
