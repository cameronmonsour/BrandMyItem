---
name: Example campaign labeling
description: Visual convention for locked homepage demo campaign cards.
---

Locked example campaigns show a neutral `Example` badge in the top-right corner of the image, while funding or countdown status remains in the top-left status badge.

**Why:** A campaign can be both an example and funded or time-limited. Combining those meanings makes the card harder to scan.

**How to apply:** Keep the example marker visually aligned with the countdown badge, but use the opposite corner and do not replace or recolor the live status indicator.
