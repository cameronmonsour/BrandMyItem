---
name: Bento status box language
description: Keep bento status treatments visually consistent and free of middle-dot separators.
---

Every highlighted state in the homepage bento uses a solid-black background, white text, a black border, and fully rounded corners. Do not use outlined or white variants for these states. Do not use middle-dot separators anywhere in the product.

**Why:** Mixed outlined, white, and black state boxes weakened the bento's visual consistency, and leading middle dots added unwanted visual noise.

**How to apply:** Reuse the same black rounded treatment for example, locked, checkout, refund, check-in, verified, funded, shipped, and delivery states. Use an em dash, comma, or separate phrase instead of `·`, `・`, or a middle-dot HTML entity.
