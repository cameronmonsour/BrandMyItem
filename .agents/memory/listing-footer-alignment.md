---
name: Listing footer alignment
description: Keep the three funding metrics aligned in compact listing cards.
---

Align the raised/goal amount, sold-count pill, and starting-price pill on one shared bottom-row centerline. Sponsor logos may sit above the starting-price pill but must not affect the amount's vertical alignment.

**Why:** Centering the amount against the full sponsor-logo stack makes it drift above the sold and price pills.

**How to apply:** Bottom-align the card footer grid, keep the right-side pill group bottom-aligned, and give the amount an alignment box matching the compact pill height. Keep every item title in the same fixed-height, single-line row with a consistent gap above the funding metrics.
