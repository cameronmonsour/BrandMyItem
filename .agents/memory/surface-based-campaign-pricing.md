---
name: Surface-based campaign pricing
description: Pricing convention for visible placement maps and seeded campaign examples.
---

Placements that have the same visible surface area must use the same price. Larger placements may carry a premium, but price differences should follow the geometry rather than the placement index or sponsor claim state.

**Why:** A seeded MacBook campaign displayed different prices on equal-size boxes, which made the marketplace look arbitrary instead of like a real campaign.

**How to apply:** When creating or updating campaign prices, group placements by their rendered area first, assign one whole-dollar tier per group, and keep the price array aligned with the rendered placement order.
