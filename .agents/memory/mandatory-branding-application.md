---
name: Mandatory branding application
description: Product rule for who applies campaign branding, the associated fee, and delivery/check-in timing.
---

BrandMyItem must apply and verify every approved sponsor mark before an item ships. Do not reintroduce an owner-applied or optional assisted-application path. Sponsor purchases include a 20% platform fee plus a required 10% branding application fee, and pre-branded delivery uses a 60-day window. The first owner check-in follows the normal cadence after shipment rather than a separate branding deadline.

**Why:** Mandatory application reduces fraud and ensures the delivered item matches the approved, funded campaign.

**How to apply:** Keep builder, checkout, listing, fulfillment, tracking, FAQ, legal, migration, and fee logic on this single path. Migrate older records away from any owner-applied state and remove obsolete branding deadlines.
