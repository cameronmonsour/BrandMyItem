---
name: Mandatory branding application
description: Product rule for who applies campaign branding, the associated fee, and delivery/check-in timing.
---

BrandMyItem must apply and verify every approved sponsor mark before an item ships. Do not reintroduce an owner-applied or optional assisted-application path. Sponsor purchase totals include 40% covering branding application, item sales tax, shipping, and handling; show only the consolidated total and “includes 40%,” never a component fee breakdown. Campaign goals and raised amounts must both include the complete charge. Pre-branded delivery uses a 60-day window, and the first owner check-in follows the normal cadence after shipment rather than a separate branding deadline.

**Why:** Mandatory application reduces fraud and ensures the delivered item matches the approved, funded campaign.

**How to apply:** Keep builder, campaign progress, checkout, listing, fulfillment, tracking, FAQ, legal, migration, and fee logic on this single path. Migrate older records away from any owner-applied state and remove obsolete branding deadlines.
