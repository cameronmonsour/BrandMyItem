---
name: Fee disclosure placement
description: Where BrandMyItem may reveal the platform's 40% amount.
---

Reveal the 40% amount only in FAQ answers. Other customer-facing surfaces should describe spot prices as all-in purchase totals and may list included services without naming the percentage or calling out a platform fee.

**Why:** The product experience should present simple final pricing while reserving the fee breakdown for users who deliberately open the FAQ.

**How to apply:** Keep the underlying pricing calculation unchanged. In bento, builder, listing, checkout, receipts, and legal/supporting copy outside FAQ accordions, use terms such as “all-in spot price,” “purchase total,” or “includes branding application, tax, shipping, and handling.”
