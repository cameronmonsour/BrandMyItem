---
name: Equal placement pricing
description: Product rule for keeping identical placement boxes identically priced to the cent.
---

Equal-size placement boxes must always receive the same price, shown to two decimal places. Never balance a total by assigning extra whole dollars to an arbitrary identical box.

**Why:** Uneven values such as $543 and $542 make visually identical sponsorship placements appear inconsistently priced. Exact cent values such as $542.50 allow the placements to remain equal while reconciling the full campaign goal.

**How to apply:** Group placements by equal surface weight before distributing the campaign goal, calculate and store prices in cents, and preserve cent precision through editing, publishing, checkout, and every placement-map label.
